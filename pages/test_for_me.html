Account & Data Deletion

At Test for me, we believe you should have complete control over your personal data. You can request the complete deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted, all your testing groups, app links, and testing progress will be permanently erased.

What happens when you delete your account?

  • Your user profile, including your email and display name, is permanently removed from our databases.
  • Any testing groups that you own are permanently deleted.
  • You will be automatically removed from any testing groups you joined as a tester.
  • Your authentication record is permanently revoked.

How to delete your account

You can delete your account directly within the application by following these steps:

  1. Open the Test for me application and log in to your account.
  2. Navigate to the Settings or Profile section.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and locate the "Delete Account" or "Manage Account" option.
  4. Tap on "Delete Account".
  5. You will be prompted with a confirmation dialog. Confirm that you wish to permanently delete your data.
  6. If required, re-authenticate (e.g., enter your password or sign in with Google again) to verify your identity.
  7. Your account and data will be immediately and permanently deleted.

Data Retention

Upon triggering the account deletion process, your data is immediately wiped from our live Firestore databases and Firebase Authentication systems. We do not retain copies of your data after the deletion process is complete.

Need Help?

If you have lost access to the app or are unable to delete your account using the steps above, you can request manual data deletion by contacting our developer support email listed on the Google Play Store. Please include the email address associated with your account.
